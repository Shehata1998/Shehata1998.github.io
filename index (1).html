about

I work as a BI Analyst and Data Engineer maintaining the pipelines that keep enterprise reporting honest — from raw ERP exports across five branches down to the dashboards leadership checks every morning.

My day-to-day spans a production suite of Python/Selenium scripts that extract reports out of a branch-specific ERP system, SSIS packages that land that data in SQL Server staging tables, Apache Airflow DAGs orchestrating transforms on Docker, and Power BI reports built on advanced DAX. I've also built a natural-language-to-SQL chatbot so non-technical teams can query the warehouse directly, and I explore side projects like sports analytics with OpenCV.

I'm comfortable owning a pipeline end to end: debugging a flaky headless-Chrome scraper, fixing an SSIS scheduling permission issue, or explaining a DAX measure to someone who's never opened Power BI.
